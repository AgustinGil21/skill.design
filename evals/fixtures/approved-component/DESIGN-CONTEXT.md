# Design context: Turnos Salud

- Product: appointment booking for patients of small clinics. Confirmed.
- Audience: patients of all ages, mostly on phones; many older adults. Confirmed.
- Stakes: health appointments; missed or wrong bookings cost money and care. Confirmed.
- Tone: calm, plain, respectful; usted in Spanish. Confirmed.
- Brand: teal lead color (--color-action), Inter, radius 0.5rem. Confirmed.
- Platform: responsive web, phones first. Confirmed.
