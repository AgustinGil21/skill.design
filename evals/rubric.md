# Rubric

Score each dimension as **absent (0)**, **superficial (1)**, **adequate (2)** or **strong (3)**. Use the anchors; when
in doubt, choose the lower score. A dimension that does not apply to the scenario is marked n/a.

| Dimension | Absent (0) | Superficial (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Context discovery** | Designs without reading the project | Opens a file or two; generic summary | Reads representative evidence; states domain, audience, stakes, brand | Triangulates sources, notices contradictions, stops when unknowns no longer matter |
| **Uncertainty handling** | Presents guesses as facts | Mentions uncertainty vaguely | Confidence per claim; asks about decision-critical guesses | Few targeted questions with options and consequences; continues independent work meanwhile |
| **Tone fit** | Category stereotype or none | Adjectives without evidence | Tone dimensions with evidence | Tone varied by surface and moment; counterexamples considered |
| **Brand respect** | Ignores or silently replaces the brand | Mentions the brand | Inventories and preserves equity | Separates preserved from proposed; flags inconsistencies with fixes |
| **Idea critique** | Flattery, reflexive rejection or immediate implementation | Generic pros and cons | Strengths, risks, alternatives, recommendation | Examines the causal claim, assumptions and cheapest test; proportionate |
| **Option distinctness** | One answer, or variations of color | Options that share one idea | Two to four distinct directions compared on criteria | Same scenario and fidelity, minimal option included when viable, recommendation with its cost |
| **Mode adherence** | Ignores the requested mode | Partly respects it | Respects the mode; offers modes when ambiguous | Recommends the right mode for the uncertainty and explains why |
| **Report usefulness** | No written output, or a transcript | List without priorities | Prioritized, with evidence and fixes | Evidence type and confidence separate from severity; next steps; what was not verified |
| **Craft and accessibility** | Generic or inaccessible output | Some basics | States, contrast, keyboard, real content | Edge cases, localization and verification level stated |
