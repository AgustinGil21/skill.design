# Survey: [topic]

**Question it answers:** [what we need to measure or size; surveys measure how many and how much, not why]
**Audience and sample:** [who receives it, how, expected responses; who is likely to be missing]
**Length:** [under 5 minutes; every question must earn its place] · **Language:** [the respondents' languages]

Rules: ask about **recent, concrete behavior** ("In the last 30 days, how many times did you…") rather than general
habits or predictions. One idea per question. Neutral wording, no leading or loaded questions. Balanced scales with a
labeled midpoint and a "Not applicable" or "Don't know" where it fits. Randomize option order where order does not
carry meaning. Pilot with three to five people first.
