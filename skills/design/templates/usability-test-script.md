## Before the session

- [ ] Prototype or build works from the starting point on the target device; test data ready (accounts, carts).
- [ ] Recording and consent set up; observers muted and briefed (no help, no reactions).
- [ ] Note-taking sheet per participant (bottom of this page).

## Introduction (read aloud, 3 minutes)

"Thanks for helping. We're testing [the product], not you: if something is hard, that's what we need to learn. Please
think aloud — say what you're looking at, what you expect and what confuses you. I may not answer questions during
the tasks, because I want to see how it works without me. You can stop at any time. Is it OK to record the screen and
audio?"

## Warm-up (2 minutes)

- How do you [activity] today? (Context only; do not discuss the design.)

## Tasks

Write tasks as goals in the person's words, without the interface's labels (not "Click 'Add payee'" but "You want to
pay your plumber €170 for the invoice you received").

During tasks: do not lead. Neutral prompts only: "What are you thinking?", "What would you do next?", "What do you
expect this to do?". If the person is stuck past the time limit, note it as a failure and move on.

## After each task

- Single Ease Question: "Overall, how easy or difficult was this task?" (1 very difficult – 7 very easy).
- "Was anything confusing or surprising?"

## Closing (5 minutes)

- "What was the most confusing part? What, if anything, worked well?"
- Thanks; incentive.
