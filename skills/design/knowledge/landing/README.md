# Landing pages

How to make landing pages that are understood in five seconds, persuade with facts, have a visual idea of their own,
move with intention and work for everyone. This folder complements the general foundations (`../00-principles.md`
onwards) and goes deeper than [product pages](../patterns/product-pages.md).

## Order of work

1. [01-strategy-and-structure.md](01-strategy-and-structure.md): goal, audience, stage of decision, objections and
   proof, structure and measurement.
2. [04-persuasive-writing.md](04-persuasive-writing.md): write the headline, subheading and every section **before**
   designing. If the copy does not persuade, the design will not save it.
3. [05-art-direction-and-creativity.md](05-art-direction-and-creativity.md): choose a visual idea (a concept, not a
   style), turn it into rules.
4. [02-hero.md](02-hero.md) and [03-sections.md](03-sections.md): resolve each block.
5. [06-motion.md](06-motion.md): purposeful motion, a budget and a reduced alternative.
6. [07-accessibility-performance-and-seo.md](07-accessibility-performance-and-seo.md): fast, readable with any
   tool, findable.
7. [08-review.md](08-review.md): review method, severity and the final checklist before publishing.

## The five questions a landing page answers

1. **What is it?** In one sentence, without jargon.
2. **For whom?** And for whom not.
3. **What do I get?** The outcome, not the feature.
4. **Why believe it?** Proof: the real product, numbers, real people.
5. **What do I do now?** One action, with the risk stated as low.

If someone who does not know the product cannot answer all five after looking at the first screen and scrolling a
little, the landing page is not done.
