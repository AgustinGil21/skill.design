# Product pages (marketing)

A product's marketing page persuades: it has to say what the product is, for whom and why now, and let people act
without friction. This file is a short orientation for when a marketing page appears inside a product task (a feature
page, a pricing teaser, an app store-style page). The complete guide is in [the landing guide](../landing/README.md).

Note the name: this file is about **marketing pages for a product**, not the product detail page of an online store
(see [commerce and maps](commerce-and-maps.md)).

## Scope

Use this file for quick decisions; use the landing guide for strategy, structure, copy, art direction, motion,
accessibility, performance and SEO in depth.

## Diagnose before designing

Before choosing a layout, answer:

- **Who arrives, and from where?** An ad, a search for a problem, a referral, an existing customer?
- **What do they already know?** Is the product category familiar, or must it be explained?
- **What is their main objection?** Price, trust, effort to switch, whether it works for their case?
- **What proof exists?** Real customers, data, demos, reviews?
- **What commitment is being asked?** Sign up free, book a demo, buy?

The answers change everything below. A visitor who knows the category needs differentiation; a visitor who does not
needs the category explained first.

## First screen (hero)

- **A headline that states the outcome**, not the category ("The whole club in one place", not "Integrated management
  platform"), usually short; clarity matters more than word count.
- **A subheading of one or two lines** saying what it does and for whom
  ([incident.io](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1f8c9d4b-9f7f-4bfa-aed3-9a849b84831e),
  [Ditto](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/9306f2a2-2b65-4f2e-bfd5-5a52790f175f)).
- **One primary action** ("Try free") and at most one secondary ("Watch demo"), with low-risk text below when it
  applies ("No card required").
- **The real product visible**: a screenshot or video of the product in use, not abstract illustrations or gradient
  blobs ([Craft](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/58c31ff2-407c-4d33-933f-3b0a08528728),
  [incident.io](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1f8c9d4b-9f7f-4bfa-aed3-9a849b84831e)). Exceptions: products with no
  meaningful interface (an API, a service) need a diagram or an example of the result instead.
- **Social proof nearby**: real logos or a verifiable figure. Nothing invented.

## Value sections

- **One idea per section**: a title that states the benefit, two or three lines, a screenshot of the specific feature
  ([Toggl](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/60d20670-e53e-4426-a9ec-2a2c8ea852c3)).
- **Use cases as tabs or an accordion** with the screenshot changing
  ([Employment Hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ea5feb3c-6436-41c9-934c-c5f4dcf2646c),
  [Miro](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/fc66d00d-aa8a-4e17-a2d6-4755259b7b4f)).
- **Testimonials with name, role, company and real photo**, with a concrete sentence (what changed), not "Excellent!".
- **Pricing** ([plans and pricing tables](plans-and-pricing-tables.md)), FAQ, and a closing section that repeats the
  primary action.

## Proof and calls to action by commitment

| Commitment asked | Proof that helps | Call to action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free sign-up | Product screenshots, ease, "no card" | "Start free" |
| Paid purchase | Pricing clarity, guarantees, reviews | "Buy now" with price |
| Sales conversation | Customer stories, security, integrations | "Book a demo" with what happens next |
| Download | Platform availability, size, ratings | Store badges or direct download |

## Signs of an autopilot landing

- Empty headlines ("Supercharge your workflow", "The future of X is here").
- Adjective triads ("Simple. Fast. Secure.") and three cards with icon + title + generic sentence.
- Purple-blue gradients, blobs, glassmorphism and glows without reason.
- Fake or generic screenshots, invented statistics, logos without permission.
- Emojis as section icons.
- Everything centered and the same size: no hierarchy, no rhythm.

See [anti-slop](../01-anti-slop.md).

## After the click

The page succeeds only if what happens after the click matches the promise: the sign-up is as easy as stated, the
demo booking confirms a time, the trial includes what was shown. Check the full journey, not only the page.

## Performance and accessibility

- First-screen load (Largest Contentful Paint) within the "good" threshold (2.5 s at the time of writing); images with
  dimensions and modern formats; video muted, with pause and a poster.
- Contrast of text on images and gradients (use a scrim or a box).
- Subtle entrance animations that respect reduced motion.
- Keyboard navigation of the menu and accordions.

See [landing accessibility, performance and SEO](../landing/07-accessibility-performance-and-seo.md).

## Worked comparison (summary)

**Weak.** "Supercharge your team ✨" over a gradient, three icon cards, "Get started".

**Strong.** "Schedule the whole clinic from one calendar" — "Online booking, reminders and patient records for
physiotherapy practices." A real screenshot of the calendar, "Start a 14-day trial · No card required", logos of real
clinics (with permission), then sections per job (booking, reminders, records) with real screenshots.
