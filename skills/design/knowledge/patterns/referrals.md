# Referral programs

Referral programs reward people for bringing others. They work when the benefit is clear, the conditions are honest,
and the invited person is treated with respect; they fail — and damage trust — when rewards are vague, conditions are
hidden, or invitations feel like spam sent on someone's behalf.

## The problem and who has it

- **Referrers** who like the product and want a reward for sharing it.
- **Invited people** who receive a link from someone they know.
- **The business**, which needs to attribute referrals correctly and prevent abuse.

When programs fail: referrers do not know whether they earned anything, invited people feel spammed, rewards are
withheld for conditions nobody explained, and support handles disputes.

## Questions to ask before deciding

- **What does each side get, and when exactly?** (On sign-up? On first purchase? After a trial?)
- **What are the eligibility conditions** (new customers only, countries, minimum spend)?
- **How is attribution decided** (link, code, time window)?
- **When can a reward be reversed** (refunds, fraud)?
- **What data about the invited person does the referrer see?**
- **Are there limits** (per month, per person)?

## Anatomy and behavior

- **The benefit in one sentence at the top, for both sides** ("You each get 15.00 EUR"), with the concrete condition
  ("when your friend lists 3 items"; [Vinted](https://mobbin.com/screens/692de8b8-7649-4c56-8ae4-e585912d3b25)).
- **How it works in numbered steps** (1. Share · 2. Your friend joins · 3. You both get rewarded;
  [Everyday Rewards](https://mobbin.com/screens/5b45125d-3393-4a34-b26f-201812793d1a),
  [Origin](https://mobbin.com/screens/a9c5671b-bbed-4572-9748-c22062228a93)).
- **The link or code visible with "Copy"** (and confirmation) in addition to the share button; a short, readable code
  ("Tap to copy"; [Setel](https://mobbin.com/screens/905abc31-8bdb-4da5-b8cd-c0646ee3edfe)). Avoid ambiguous characters
  (O and 0, I and l) in codes people type.
- **Status of invitations**: in progress · completed · earned, with pending items and an explained empty state ("They'll
  appear when your friends sign up"; [Trip.com](https://mobbin.com/screens/3ab7d6b1-a41e-4186-b425-2f5c11f57408),
  [Greenlight](https://mobbin.com/screens/c7f95630-20aa-4214-8cf4-02b5e359361e)).
- **Reward tiers visible** when they grow with each referral
  ([Stake](https://mobbin.com/screens/3cd429b3-0a14-46c6-ab56-7af439778538)).
- **Limits and terms summarized** before the link to full conditions (monthly cap, countries, deadlines).
- **Inviting contacts is optional** and asks for permission separately; never send invitations without the person
  seeing exactly to whom and what will be sent.

## The states of a reward

| State | Meaning | Display |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invited | Link shared or sent | "Invited 3 days ago" (only if the referrer sent it directly) |
| Joined | Signed up, condition pending | "Ana joined · needs to make a first order" |
| Qualified | Condition met, reward pending | "Reward on the way · available on 20 Oct" |
| Earned | Reward available | "15.00 EUR added to your balance" |
| Reversed | Reward removed (refund, fraud) | "Reward reversed because the order was refunded" |
| Expired | Condition not met in time | "Ana's invitation expired on 1 Nov" |

Explain each state and its timeline; disputes come from unexplained states.

## Privacy of the invited person

- Show the referrer only what is necessary (a first name or "1 friend joined"), not purchases or personal data.
- Let invited people opt out of being shown.
- The invitation message is sent in the referrer's name only with their consent, and says who is inviting and why.

## The invited person's experience

- A landing page that names the referrer and the reward, in the invited person's language.
- The reward applied automatically, with confirmation, not a code to remember.
- No pressure tactics (fake deadlines).

## Copy

- Concrete amounts and conditions.
- Status explanations with dates.
- Invitation messages editable by the referrer, written to sound personal, not like an ad.
- Amounts and currencies localized; conditions that differ by country stated per country.

## Common mistakes and why they happen

- **Vague rewards** ("Get rewarded!").
- **Hidden conditions** discovered after the friend joins.
- **Contact uploads by default.**
- **Rewards reversed without explanation.**
- **Exposing invited people's activity.**

## Worked comparison

**Weak.** "Invite friends and earn rewards!" A share button. Weeks later the referrer asks support why they got
nothing; the friend's first order was below an unmentioned minimum.

**Strong.** "You both get 15.00 EUR when your friend's first order is over 30.00 EUR, within 30 days." A list shows
"Ana joined · first order pending (expires 12 Nov)". When the order qualifies: "Reward available on 20 Oct (after the
return window)".

## How to verify and measure

- **Measure** shares, joins, qualified referrals, reversals, support disputes, and the quality of referred customers
  (retention), not only volume.
- **Test** comprehension of conditions with referrers and invited people.
