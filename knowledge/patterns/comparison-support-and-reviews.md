# Comparison tables, support requests and reviews

Three patterns where people evaluate or report: **comparison tables** to choose between plans or products,
**support requests** to get a problem solved, and **reviews and ratings** to share an experience. Each depends on
fairness and context: a comparison that omits the decisive attribute, a support form that makes people repeat
themselves, a review prompt that only appears after good experiences.

Complements [plans and pricing tables](plans-and-pricing-tables.md).

## The problem and who has it

- **People choosing** between options with many attributes.
- **People with a problem** who need help without explaining it three times.
- **People who used something** and are asked to rate it — and the people who read those ratings to decide.

## Questions to ask before deciding

- **Which attributes actually discriminate** between options for this audience?
- **How many options and attributes**, and on which screen sizes?
- **Can self-service solve the support problem**, or does it need a human with context?
- **What exactly is being rated**: the product, the delivery, the seller, the experience?
- **Who will read reviews**, and how will they be moderated?

## Comparison tables

### Choosing attributes

- Start from the decision: which attributes change which option is right for whom?
- Put **equivalent attributes** side by side in the same units ("30 orders per hour" vs. "600"), not one in hours and
  another per day.
- **Include costs** that matter (setup fees, overages), not only features.
- **Do not favor an option by omission**: if the recommended plan is weak on an attribute people care about, show it.

### Anatomy and behavior

- **Sticky column headers** while scrolling, with name, price and button in each; in long tables the action stays at
  hand ([Calendly](https://mobbin.com/screens/c3a7b2d3-de35-4e07-8a04-6e23f3b6d0a6),
  [Squarespace](https://mobbin.com/screens/3b274648-fcb3-4050-81df-607aea37a1e6)).
- **One highlighted column** (Recommended / Most chosen) with a background across the whole column
  ([Asana](https://mobbin.com/screens/5c6d27f8-ff4f-47da-a25c-bb448c5e1129),
  [Clockwise](https://mobbin.com/screens/6dfb7e0e-f5aa-43f5-a098-67de4cf6b776)) — and a reason for the
  recommendation.
- **The current plan marked** ("Current plan") for existing customers
  ([7shifts](https://mobbin.com/screens/0a9b21b2-170c-4eb4-b758-ead8060acd38)).
- **Rows grouped by theme, collapsible**, with tabs or jump links per group (Scheduling · Contacts · Payments;
  Calendly).
- **Concrete values instead of only checks** when they differ ("30 orders per hour" vs. "600";
  [Toggl Track](https://mobbin.com/screens/d08c4a1a-c25b-4730-b8f5-e13b039ab997)); a dash with a label for "not
  included", not an empty cell that looks like an error.
- **An info button per row** explaining the feature without leaving the table.
- **"Everything in X, plus:"** in summary cards to avoid repeating lists
  ([Chatbase](https://mobbin.com/screens/224aa534-d8b7-4059-952f-6f03e35256c3),
  [Airwallex](https://mobbin.com/screens/03a25b36-417f-4e01-ab26-36ea3caa709d)) — but cumulative lists demand
  memory; keep the full table available.
- **Monthly / annual** with the saving in money or percentage and the annual total.
- **Taxes clarified** ("Prices exclude taxes", Squarespace), per region.
- **On mobile**: choose two options to compare, or one column at a time with a selector — never a cramped five-column
  table.

### Honest but useless comparisons

A comparison can be accurate and still unhelpful: forty rows of checks where every option has every feature, and the
three rows that differ buried in the middle. Show differences first, or offer "Show only differences".

## Support requests

### Self-service and escalation

- **Topic first, then text**: category and subcategory, then **suggest articles** that solve it before sending ("See
  this article: Change your school's name"; [Teachable](https://mobbin.com/screens/d8814888-0b2b-494a-942e-37d79ba866f0);
  suggestions while typing, [Savee](https://mobbin.com/screens/3aec0efa-9994-4c05-a78e-5814494fb42d)).
- **Escalation without repetition**: when a chat or bot hands over to a human, the human sees everything already said.
- **Continuity across channels**: a request started by email and continued by chat is one case.

### Anatomy and behavior

- **Guiding questions as labels** ("What failed and when? What were you doing?") instead of "Description"
  ([Snowflake](https://mobbin.com/screens/a593debd-e1e0-4d64-b62a-cb7797c5f540)).
- **An explicit request type** (Problem · Question · Suggestion;
  [StackAI](https://mobbin.com/screens/43ef885c-5877-4ae2-93b1-88fc93383344)) and **severity** when there are service
  levels.
- **Attachments with limits stated** (types, size, count) and how to get useful ones ("How to generate a HAR file";
  [LangChain](https://mobbin.com/screens/667836b7-39e3-4762-9213-6ff1e5b0d74c)).
- **Technical context attached automatically** (version, browser, operation ID), with notice, not requested by hand.
- **Warn what not to include** ("Don't paste passwords or personal data") and what is shared on sending
  ([WhatsApp](https://mobbin.com/screens/5a532e09-ba70-4db4-8931-a993303ce3b5)).
- **A minimum length with the reason** ("Describe it in at least 10 characters") instead of just disabling the button.
- **On sending**: a case number, when to expect a reply and through which channel. If sending fails, **keep what was
  written** and offer retry (anti-example: a generic red error with nothing else, StackAI).
- **Frequent resources beside the form** (Snowflake) and another channel at hand (chat, status page).
- **Support in the person's language**, or a clear note of supported languages and hours in their time zone.

## Reviews and ratings

### What is being rated?

Separate experiences that have different owners:

| Aspect | Example |
| --- | --- |
| The product | "The jacket fits as expected" |
| The delivery | "Arrived late" |
| The seller or provider | "Answered quickly" |
| The service | "The driver was friendly" |

A one-star product review caused by a late delivery hurts the product unfairly and helps nobody choose.

### Anatomy and behavior

- **The rating first and alone**, large; on selecting, a word naming it ("Love it!";
  [Under Armour](https://mobbin.com/screens/96760525-8f71-4e6e-a0e1-40496c7a5355)).
- **Guidance on what to tell**, by what is rated ("Tell us about taste, portion and packaging";
  [Glovo](https://mobbin.com/screens/45e0b0b1-885d-45be-8a41-e7caf5be5a11),
  [Grab](https://mobbin.com/screens/d3777084-5b08-4e39-a80f-ee196d68083a)).
- **Aspect chips** ("Fair portions", "Well cooked") for people who do not want to write
  ([Postmates](https://mobbin.com/screens/2aa3e28e-c1cb-4743-b3f4-a70fa8ec8836)).
- **Rate per item** when there are several, each with its own text and photos
  ([Blinkit](https://mobbin.com/screens/6fe81e51-af6c-4bf1-8cb1-33972e198fbd)).
- **Optional photos with a counter** ("0 of 5") and remove each.
- **Who sees it and under what name** ("Posted as Sam L · Everyone";
  [DoorDash](https://mobbin.com/screens/355cab03-eac2-4def-9435-e91209dd82ca)).
- **Length requirements stated up front**, with a counter ("Between 50 and 1,000 characters · 0/1000";
  [UNIQLO](https://mobbin.com/screens/1e46ef95-e3e2-4879-92f1-2120103dc6bc)); ask for the minimum required.
- **Integrity**: declare that it is one's own experience when it matters
  ([Tripadvisor](https://mobbin.com/screens/f322fc25-fed6-4a54-a8e0-f8aeca273fd4)).
- **Ask at the right moment** (after use or delivery), once, with "Not now".

### Publishing, editing and moderation

- Say whether reviews are published immediately or after moderation, and why one might be rejected.
- Allow editing and deleting one's own review.
- Show reviews in context for readers: verified purchase, date, the variant purchased, the language (with optional
  translation).
- **Sampling bias**: prompting only after positive interactions inflates ratings; prompt consistently.
- **Never fabricate or filter reviews deceptively**; legal requirements in many regions prohibit it.

## Accessibility specifics

- Comparison tables are real tables with row and column headers; checks have text equivalents.
- Support forms follow form accessibility; case numbers are selectable.
- Star ratings are radio groups with labels ("4 out of 5 stars"), operable by keyboard.

## Copy

- Comparison rows in the people's vocabulary.
- Support labels as guiding questions.
- Review prompts specific to what is rated, in the person's language.

## Common mistakes and why they happen

- **Tables that favor an option by omission.**
- **Forty rows of identical checks.**
- **Support forms that lose text on error.**
- **Escalations that start from zero.**
- **Mixing delivery and product ratings.**
- **Biased review prompts.**

## Worked comparison

**Same situation:** a customer's order of three items arrived late with one item damaged.

**Weak.** One prompt: "Rate your order ★★★★★" with a text box. The customer gives one star; the product's rating
drops; the delivery partner never learns about the delay; the damaged item requires a separate support form that
asks for the order number again.

**Strong.** "How was your delivery?" (on time / late, with chips) and "Rate your items" per product; "One item
arrived damaged? Report it" opens a support request pre-filled with the order and the item, with photo upload and the
expected reply time.

## How to verify and measure

- **Comparisons**: time to choose, plan changes after purchase (wrong choice), use of "show differences".
- **Support**: self-service resolution, repeat contacts, time to resolution, satisfaction after resolution.
- **Reviews**: response rate, distribution by prompt moment, moderation outcomes.
