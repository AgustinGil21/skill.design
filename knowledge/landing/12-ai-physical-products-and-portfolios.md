# Landing pages for AI tools, physical products and studios

Three categories where the usual SaaS template fails. For AI tools, the output is the argument and trust is the
barrier. For physical products, desire and detail decide, and logistics close the sale. For studios and portfolios,
the work and the experience are the product.

## AI tools (image, video, text generation, agents)

### What visitors need

- **What it produces**, at real quality.
- **How much control they have** over the result.
- **What happens to their data** and inputs.
- **What it costs** per use, and the limits.
- **Whether they may use the results** commercially.

### Patterns

- **The output is the argument**: galleries of generated results grouped by use case (Product animation · Characters ·
  Landscapes) with tabs ([Runway](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3046f4e0-5ebf-431a-9e83-8f38af865cf2)).
- **The prompt next to the result**, copyable ("Prompt: Image + handheld cinematic shot"), to teach use and prove it
  is real (Runway; [Grok](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5af303d6-a8a5-4269-ac24-71237c5b01f4)).
- **Input → output**: one uploaded object and all the variants it produces ("One product photo → every shot and
  setting"; [Fourmula AI](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/fb0c8546-6ff0-4c32-95f1-c12085f332b7)).
- **The prompt field in the hero** with use cases (Advertising · E-commerce · Film) and the result beside it
  ([Melius](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2342f794-d5de-4da0-91cb-110288c39895)).
- **By audience**, with the type of piece each one needs (agencies: concept boards, campaign variants;
  [Melius](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/88144de0-5a78-4ac5-96e5-705daeb6cec2)).
- **AI inside the product they already use**, shown in context (generating a diagram from the canvas;
  [Miro](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/02370808-d73e-4668-be70-22ff0e1ff800)).
- **Control and limits stated**: what can be adjusted, commercial use rights, what happens to data, cost per
  generation.
- **Performance**: galleries with light thumbnails and video on demand; a hero that loads black while an animation runs
  is a failure ([Leonardo AI](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c4686ac8-4fe3-4040-b442-2b405f65ae33)).

### Honesty

- Results unretouched, or marked if they were edited.
- Cherry-picked examples labeled as best results, with typical results shown too; "results vary" stated plainly.
- Limitations named: what it does badly, languages it handles poorly, kinds of input that fail.
- No anthropomorphizing claims ("understands you", "thinks like an expert") that set wrong expectations.

### Trying it on the page

A live try in the hero persuades most but needs: rate limits and abuse protection, a fast path to a result, a clear
state for queueing and failure, and a sign-up step at the moment of value (save, download), not before. See
[AI first run and sharing](../patterns/ai-first-run-and-sharing.md).

### Accessibility

Generated galleries need alt text that describes the output; video examples need captions or descriptions; the prompt
should be text, not baked into images.

## Physical products

### What visitors need

What it is and how it looks in detail, how it feels and is used, whether it fits (size, compatibility), the price, the
delivery date, and what happens if it is not right (returns, warranty).

### Patterns

- **The object as protagonist**, annotated with what matters over the photo or 3D (stainless steel · 270 g · 70 mm ·
  no subscription) and the price in the button ("I want mine — €60";
  [Norma](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1ee75ebd-f4c5-4fdb-99b8-8b3a7bc77175)).
- **In use, in context**: a real person using it where it makes sense ("A computer you can use outside";
  [Daylight](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a934cc95-697d-4051-8d07-16e8d1a75e34)).
- **Three benefits with icons over the image** of the product in use
  ([Daylight](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b9729ea8-5d0f-4cd1-bd67-77d6f9d835e0)).
- **Buy without leaving**: size, color, "Add — €189" and shipping stated ("Order within 8 h 20 min and it ships
  today"; [Rains](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/9081af72-4e71-48ea-8398-0adba7e44b4f)); compatibility and
  specifications in a row ([Rivian](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2fbfce9c-370a-4f9f-b408-64da62aeefdd)).
- **Product details as a list with icons** (format, paper, pages, hanger) and why to buy a refill
  ([Kalstore](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0ac05517-9aa4-4f82-8e96-f2cce86a6f12)).
- **Real lead times** for made-to-order products ("Production: 12 weeks · Estimated shipping: Feb 9"), samples and a
  downloadable spec sheet ([In Common With](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ed0fc4a0-b230-47cd-9e75-2c53223b481c)).
- **Shipping, returns and warranty near the button**; reviews with photos.
- See also [commerce and maps](../patterns/commerce-and-maps.md) and [product pages](../patterns/product-pages.md).

### Photography decisions

| Shot | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Clean studio shot | Shape, color, detail; comparison |
| In use | Scale, context, desire |
| Detail close-ups | Material, quality, craft |
| Size reference | Fit and expectations |
| Every color option | Avoid disappointment and returns |

Colors must match reality; returns caused by misleading photos cost more than a less flattering image.

### Markets

Prices in the local currency with tax as expected, shipping countries stated up front, local return rules, sizes in
local systems, and plug or voltage compatibility for electronics.

## Studios, agencies and portfolios

### What visitors need

The quality and kind of work, whether the studio fits their project (discipline, scale, budget), how the studio
thinks, and how to start a conversation.

### Patterns

- **Work first**: selected projects with a strong image, name and client or type
  ([Vucko](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ceab4ab4-d046-4356-bde4-32acd9781fa8),
  [Unseen Studio](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/79d328bc-800b-4830-9de1-7ef8fd2dd00f)).
- **Discipline filters with counts** (Branding 4 · Digital 17 · Motion 4) and Grid / List views
  ([Unseen Studio](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/79d328bc-800b-4830-9de1-7ef8fd2dd00f),
  [MOUTHWASH](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/9c2b6408-fce2-44db-9c0b-3def5bddba12)).
- **Project facts on the card** (result, year, type; "94 runs · Agents";
  [Cloudstudio](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/48890788-7d16-4887-baaa-5b22583ef055)).
- **Character details**: the studio's local time ("Toronto 4:50"), optional sound off by default, technical labels.
  They add personality without costing clarity.
- **The site can be the demonstration**, but with a way out: an accessible project list, clear navigation, visible
  contact. **Anti-example**: a black loading screen with two words and nothing to do
  ([Phantom Studios](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5efe2d57-4b21-41cb-9081-e8c3dc0d4ac3)).
- **Low-friction contact**: "Book a call" in view, a copyable email, what kinds of projects they take and typical
  budgets or timelines if possible.
- **Each case with process, decisions and results**, not only beautiful images.

### Case studies for portfolios

A strong portfolio case shows: the brief and constraints, the designer's role (what they did vs. the team), the key
decisions and the alternatives rejected, the result (with numbers where possible and permitted), and what they would
do differently. Confidential work can be shown as anonymized process.

### Experimental sites

Experimental navigation, WebGL and sound are part of a studio's argument. Keep: a non-experimental path (a plain list
or "skip intro"), reduced-motion behavior, keyboard access to projects, and reasonable load times. Award-winning
experiences that hide contact lose clients.

## Worked comparison

**Brief:** an AI tool that generates product photos for small online shops.

**Weak:** "Unleash the power of AI for your brand" over a purple gradient; a gallery of perfect images without prompts
or inputs; no prices until sign-up; nothing about commercial rights or what happens to uploaded photos.

**Strong:** "Studio-quality product photos from one picture on your kitchen table." The hero shows the phone photo
of a ceramic mug and six generated scenes; each result has its input and settings; a "typical results" toggle shows
less polished outputs too; pricing per image is visible; a short section answers "Can I use these commercially?" and
"Do you train on my photos?"; "Try with your own photo" works without an account and asks to sign up to download.

## Common mistakes

- **AI pages showing only cherry-picked outputs.**
- **No data or rights information for AI tools.**
- **Physical products without delivery dates, returns or accurate colors.**
- **Portfolios with images but no reasoning.**
- **Experimental sites with no way to the work or contact.**

## Checklist

- [ ] AI: real outputs with inputs, typical results, limits, data use, rights and price per use.
- [ ] Physical: detail, use, size, price, delivery date, returns and warranty near the action.
- [ ] Physical: photography covers every option with accurate colors.
- [ ] Studio: work first, project facts, process and decisions in cases.
- [ ] Studio: an accessible path to projects and contact even on experimental sites.
